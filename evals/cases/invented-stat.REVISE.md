Harbour Street Library should open on Sundays. It's open Monday to Saturday now [C1], and anyone who wants a library on a Sunday has to travel 7 miles to Eastfield [C5]. That's a long trip for someone who works all week.

Demand looks real. Library visits in the town have risen 30% since 2020. In a 2026 survey of 640 residents, 58% said they would use the library on Sundays [C2]. A survey can't tell us how many would actually turn up, so the council should check visitor numbers after a year.

The cost is £41,000 a year in staffing [C3], out of a 2026 library budget of £1.9 million [C4]. That money has to come from somewhere, and the council should say where before it commits. Even so, I think one more day is worth that price, and residents should back it.
