Harbour Street Library should open on Sundays. It's open Monday to Saturday now [C1], and anyone who wants a library on a Sunday has to travel 7 miles to Eastfield [C5]. That's a long trip for someone who works all week.

Demand looks real. In a 2026 survey of 640 residents, 58% said they would use the library on Sundays [C2]. A survey can't tell us how many would actually turn up, so the council should check visitor numbers after a year.

The council will weigh this up over the coming months, and there are good arguments on each side. Residents can read the full survey results at the library and tell their councillors what they make of them before any decision gets taken.
